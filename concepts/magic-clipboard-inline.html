Magic Clipboard

The magic clipboard adds AI-assisted dictation and paste-to-fill on top of any form. Bind it to a <tiro-form-filler> with the for attribute — dictate or paste free text, and it writes structured answers straight into the form's fields.

Inline embed

Place <tiro-magic-clipboard> next to the form it should fill and point its for attribute at the form-filler's id. That's the whole integration.

Preview
Source
<!-- Load the Tiro Web SDK -->
<script src="https://cdn.tiro.health/.../tiro-web-sdk.iife.js"></script>

<!-- The form to fill -->
<tiro-form-filler id="report">
  <script type="application/fhir+json" slot="questionnaire">
  { /* Questionnaire with Findings + Conclusion */ }
  </script>
</tiro-form-filler>

<!-- Magic clipboard, bound to the form via `for` -->
<tiro-magic-clipboard for="report">
  <button type="submit">Autofill</button>
</tiro-magic-clipboard>
